I am a knowledge junkie

When I was little, my mom used to say I would be an inventor. It turned out I became an engineer. I like understanding and making sense of complex things. I am also the queen of documentaries. Every once in a while I will binge watch many videos about a specific topic. I recently spent around two weeks looking into mushroom production!

I am very passionate about aquaponics and did my master's regarding this. I am currently in my doctorate digging a bit deeper into it and want to make a practical tool for people to use. Feel free to contact me if you are curious!

In work I tend to try to automate things. I learned MsAccess so that I wouldn't have to do repetitive work when I was a trainee. Ever since then I have learned R, Python and other programming stuff (I made this site by myself using html).

I want to eventually join/create an incubator for sustainable business benefiting from sinergies - connecting "wastes" to inputs. The idea is to start with eager young people (not only in university, but even earlier, from poor communities) and create an innovation hub that creates working companies.

In case you are here for the work stuff

Currently working as Supply Chain Design COE Modelling Manager for Cargill where I lead and create optimizations to aid in business' decision-making process. I previously worked as the senior specialist that created the Asset Footprint area at Yara Brasil, modelling and analyzing the logistics and production network for investments and divestments and Mergers and Acquisitions.

Previously worked as a senior business consultant in value chain planning and performance improvement. We integrated planning process from the strategy to the execution. Consulting career was focused on major business transformation projects in the agricultural, chemical, food, ceramic, mining and clothing industries.

Graduated in Industrial Engineering from the Federal University of São Carlos - UFSCar with a yearlong exchange program in ENITIAA (currently ONIRIS). I also have a master's degree in agribusiness from Fundação Getúlio Vargas with Esalq / USP and recently started my PhD at Wageningen.

Have a CPIM certificate (Certificate in Production and Inventory Management) from APICS, a Business Management course at the São Paulo School of Business, a certification in the Harvard Manage Mentor Program and a course in Consumer Anthropology by the Perestroika School.

The tools that have helped me throughout my career have been: Supply Chain & Data Guru, OP2B, Tableau, PowerBI, MS suite (including Access and macros), R studio, Python (Jupyter notebook), VS Studio and Figma. I am also currently learning IBM Cloud development.
